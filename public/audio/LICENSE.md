# Starter audio

The six WAV files are original synthesized recordings generated for this project by `generate.py`. They use no sampled recordings. The compositions are Twinkle, Twinkle, Little Star; Frère Jacques; Ode to Joy (Ludwig van Beethoven); Happy Birthday to You; When the Saints Go Marching In; and London Bridge Is Falling Down.
